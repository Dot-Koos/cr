A different way to connect

It is not about winning the argument. It is about understanding the person.

The Imago Dialogue is built around three simple practices: mirroring what you hear, validating your partner's experience, and responding with empathy.

The Imago Approach

Understand the patterns. Rediscover the connection.

Imago Relationship Therapy, developed by Harville Hendrix and Helen LaKelly Hunt, invites couples to look beneath the surface of conflict and become curious about one another's inner worlds.

During your intensive, the therapist facilitates purposeful dialogue and helps you approach difficult conversations with greater care and understanding.

  • Mirroring — listening and reflecting what you hear.
  • Validation — recognising your partner's perspective.
  • Empathy — making room for your partner's feelings.

Good to know

Questions you might have.

Taking time for your relationship is a meaningful decision. Here is what to know before you enquire.

What is an Imago Couples Intensive?

It is focused, therapist-facilitated time for one couple. Rather than a group workshop, the attention is on your relationship, your conversations, and the patterns you want to understand or change.

What is the difference between in-person and online?

The therapeutic approach is the same. Both are packaged as 15 hours of sessions. The in-person intensive takes place in Namibia, at NAD 14,999 from Monday to Wednesday or NAD 22,999 from Thursday to Saturday. The online intensive lets you meet from home, at NAD 41,999 from Monday to Wednesday or NAD 61,999 from Thursday to Saturday, and begins with a free one-hour Clarity Session.

What about privacy and confidentiality?

What you share stays within the therapeutic setting. Your therapist will explain confidentiality, record-keeping and any limits to confidentiality before you begin.

Is the online Clarity Session really free?

Yes. The one-hour Clarity Session comes before your 15 online hours and costs nothing. It is simply a chance to meet the therapist and see whether the intensive feels right. There is no obligation to continue, and the fee applies only if you both choose to go ahead.

How long is the Intensive?

Both the in-person and online intensives are packaged as 15 hours of sessions. The online intensive also begins with a free one-hour Clarity Session. How those hours are scheduled is confirmed with the therapist before booking.
